# Source research and version decisions

Checked on 2026-10-27 (Asia/Hong_Kong). Three complete, directly verified recipes form the first release. Twelve is a ceiling, not a reason to publish incomplete research.

## Published recipes

### Tetsu Kasuya — HARIO teaching version

https://www.hario-europe.com/blogs/hario-community/v60-ambassadors-tetsu-kasuya

The source identifies a ceramic V60, 20 g coarse coffee, 300 mL water at 92°C, and a 3:30 finish. The five additions are 50/70/60/60/60 mL at 0:00/0:45/1:30/2:10/2:40. Early visual concepts used different late-pour times; those were corrected to the primary source in the website. This version is not presented as a transcription of the competition performance. Publication date, coffee and water composition are not supplied.

### James Hoffmann — one-cup V60

https://www.hario-usa.com/blogs/recipes-and-more-from-friends/james-hoffmann-1-cup-v60-technique

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1oB1oDrDkHM

The creator's video description and HARIO's transcription agree: 15 g coffee, 250 g soft filtered water, medium-fine grind. Freshly boiled water is qualified for lighter roasts; it is not silently replaced with 100°C. Preserve the swirl and pause intervals as distinct instructions. Approximate 3:00 drawdown is guidance, not a fixed required finish time. Video published 2022-11-22.

### Scott Rao — Pulsar starting recipe

https://www.scottrao.com/blog/pulsar-recipe

Published 2023-09-14. Coffee dose is 25 g and mass ratio is 17:1 water:coffee. Total water 425 g is a transparent calculation, not a separately quoted measurement. Source specifies freshly boiled water, 75 g first addition, valve control and later pulse pours maintaining 1–2 cm above the bed. Exact later pulse amounts and times are not invented. The overview's 45–60 second bloom differs from the technique's 45–50 second restart; retain and explain both. Brew time is a 3:30–4:30 range.

## Editorial policy

- Use independent short summaries, original facts and links rather than copying articles or video transcripts.
- Preserve units, ranges, conditional instructions and missing information.
- Localized strings never override canonical recipe numbers.
- Nine-language translations are independently prepared and terminology-checked; native-speaker review is a future editorial improvement, not a claimed acceptance result.
- Images are independently generated editorial illustrations, not product-accuracy evidence or author-supplied photography.

### Data and translation changes

- Each step may retain its own published `g` or `mL` unit. Validation compares totals only when units match; no assumed water-density conversion.
- Steps with unknown additions retain `null`; cumulative targets remain separate. The timeline marks unknown additions with a dash, and the instructions explain what is missing.
- Temperature has an explicit boiling/unknown mode. New sources that merely say hot water or give no temperature display unknown rather than boiling.
- Competition context, original bean/water/filter conditions and recipe-specific valve/pouring instructions are localized in all nine languages.
- April’s legacy source is still unavailable (404 checked again). Francesco Sanapo’s official HARIO UK article explicitly says dose/time “pending”; it stays unpublished rather than being filled from third-party recipes.
